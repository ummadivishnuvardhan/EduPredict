# Dataset

EduPredict includes `student-mat.csv`, the mathematics subset of the official UCI Student Performance dataset. It contains 395 records and uses semicolons as field separators. We use the real UCI column names. `G3` is the final mathematics grade on a 0–20 scale and is the prediction target; `G1` and `G2` are first- and second-period grades.

Source: Paulo Cortez (2008), [UCI Student Performance](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/320/student-performance), DOI [10.24432/C5TG7T](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5TG7T). UCI lists the dataset under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). Retain attribution and the license link when redistributing or adapting the data.

This historical observational sample describes Portuguese secondary-school students. It is small and may not represent other schools, populations, or current conditions. It does not establish causes or intervention effectiveness. The 10/20 review threshold is illustrative and must be aligned with local grading rules.
